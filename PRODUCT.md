# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

PaperX is for new and developing Indian-market traders who want to practise investing with real market prices before risking real money. They use it to discover stocks, build a watchlist, place simulated trades, and understand portfolio performance.

## Product Purpose

PaperX combines live Upstox market data with a separate paper wallet and simulated order engine. Success means a user can understand the market, place a practice trade confidently, and see the result without confusing paper activity with their real Upstox account.

## Positioning

Real Indian market data, zero real-money risk.

## Brand Personality

Confident, focused, and transparent. PaperX should feel like a trustworthy trading workspace: fast to scan, calm under dense information, and explicit about what is live data versus simulated activity.

## Anti-references

Avoid casino-like trading visuals, excessive neon, fake market metrics, decorative animation, and interfaces that imitate another broker’s brand. Avoid terminal styling when it makes ordinary controls harder to understand.

## Design Principles

- Put the user’s current market and portfolio state first.
- Use familiar brokerage patterns so users can act without relearning navigation.
- Label live data, delayed data, and simulated actions honestly.
- Prefer useful density over oversized cards or decorative whitespace.
- Make loading, empty, disconnected, and error states instructive.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic controls, visible keyboard focus, text labels alongside color-coded gains and losses, reduced-motion-safe transitions, and touch-friendly controls on smaller screens.
